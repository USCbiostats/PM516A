# Red Flag Review Scenarios Overview

| Scenario | Name | Complexity | Major Flags | Points of Emphasis |
|-----------|------|------------|-------------|--------------------|
| 1 | Epidural Steroid Injection Study | ⭐⭐☆☆☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/MULTIPLE_TESTING-9D4EDD)<br> ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/NO_CONTROL-D7263D) | - Several hypothesis tests were run, inflating the family-wise Type I error rate<br>- No control group means observed change could be due to placebo or Hawthorne effects<br>- Intraindividual correlation ignored; mixed-effects or GEE frameworks preferred<br>- Missingness could bias results (e.g., worse outcomes more likely to drop out)<br>- Clinical significance should be examined |
| 2 | Mental Health Attitudes Intervention | ⭐⭐☆☆☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/NO_CONTROL-D7263D) | - Observed change may reflect testing effects, social desirability bias, or regression to the mean<br>- Outcome reflects momentary shift; no durability assessment<br>- 5-point Likert analyzed via t-test (generally robust, not inherently wrong)<br>- Clinical significance of 0.13-point increase should be evaluated<br>- Web-recruited volunteers may limit generalizability |
| 3 | Orthopaedic Knee Surgery vs. Historical Benchmark | ⭐⭐⭐☆☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/INFLATING_UNITS-E9C46A) | - Recovery times from same patient likely correlated, inflating effective sample size<br>- Historical controls may differ in population, care standards, inclusion criteria<br>- Retrospective design introduces selection bias<br>- Historical mean lacks uncertainty estimate<br>- Small sample size (n=28)<br>- Should reduce analysis to patient-level unit |
| 4 | Subgroup Re-Analysis Based on Baseline Values | ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/POST--HOC_DECISIONS-5E60CE) | - Subgroups identified by baseline data may introduce bias<br>- Extreme baseline values may naturally regress downward<br>- No control group to distinguish regression to the mean from treatment effect<br>- Small post-hoc subgroup sample<br>- Conclusion contains causal language; more accurate phrasing needed<br>- Could analyze full sample with baseline interaction term |
| 5 | Randomized Trial with Non-Significant Result | ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/OVERINTERPRETING_NONSIGNIFICANCE-2A9D8F) | - "No evidence of effect" ≠ "evidence of no effect"<br>- Confidence interval suggests potentially meaningful benefit<br>- Clinical significance of 0.7-point difference should be examined<br>- Power analysis needed to assess detectable effect size<br>- Proper equivalence or non-inferiority framework required for such conclusion |
| 6 | Green Jelly Beans | ⭐⭐☆☆☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/P--HACKING-6A4C93)<br> ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/MULTIPLE_TESTING-9D4EDD)<br> | - Multiple hypotheses tested; likely Type I error<br>- Analyses should be labeled exploratory<br>- Correlation does not imply causation<br>- Effect size and variability metrics should accompany p-values<br>- No biological plausibility established |
| 7 | Between-Group Comparisons | ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/COMPARISON_WITHOUT_TEST-F4A261) | - Difference in significance ≠ significant difference<br>- Study may lack power to detect 2 mmHg difference<br>- p=0.09 indicates insufficient evidence, not absence of effect<br>- Confidence intervals should accompany mean differences<br>- Should test formal interaction or between-group comparison directly |
| 8 | Stress Reduction and Weekly Anxiety | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/SPURIOUS_EFFECTS-118AB2)<br> ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/CORRELATION_&_CAUSATION-2E8B57) | - App use not randomized; may vary with time-related factors<br>- Anxiety may influence app usage (reverse causation)<br>- Repeated measures dependence not modeled<br>- Aggregated group-level data risks ecological fallacy<br>- Causal language used in conclusion<br>- Temporal lag models could be explored |
| 9 | Small Sample Correlation Study | ⭐⭐⭐⭐☆ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/SMALL_SAMPLE-F46036)<br> ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/OVERINTERPRETING_NONSIGNIFICANCE-2A9D8F) | - Large effect estimates unstable in small samples<br>- Confidence interval around r=0.65 likely wide<br>- Pilot studies generate hypotheses; causal claims inappropriate<br>- Cross-sectional design limits causal inference<br>- Small sample susceptible to influential outliers |
| 10 | Workshop Participation and Exam Performance | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | ![Static Badge](https://img.shields.io/badge/SPURIOUS_EFFECTS-118AB2) | - Simpson’s Paradox indicates confounding<br>- School confounds relationship (higher pass rates and attendance at School A)<br>- Self-selection bias present<br>- Causal language inappropriate given confounding<br>- Formal interaction by school should be tested |
